Privacy

What we hold

Your email address, business name, and the receipt files you upload along with the description you give each one.

Why we hold it

Your email identifies your account and is how we reach you. Your business name heads your receipts list. The files is the service itself. The description is a short note that helps with reminder of what it is, keeping them is the whole point of the tool.

We do not sell or share any of it.

Where it is kept

Files are stored privately and are never published to a public address. They are sent only to you, over an authenticated request, after we check the receipt belongs to your account. Your PIN is stored only as a hash, so nobody here can read it.

Approval

Accounts are approved by hand. When you request access we will email you a few questions about your business before switching the account ON. That exchange is how we know who is using the tool.

Deleting things

Deleting a receipt removes both the record and the stored file. To remove your whole account and everything in it, email hello@tech-expat.com.
