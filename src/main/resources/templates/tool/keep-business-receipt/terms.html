What this is

Keep Business Receipt is an early tool, run by one person, for keeping photographs of business receipts somewhere you can find them again.

Keep your own copies

Please do not let this be the only place a receipt exists. It is a convenience, not a system of record, and you should keep your originals or your own copies of anything you rely on for tax, audit or accounting.

Your account

Accounts are approved by hand and are for the business you told us about. Keep your PIN to yourself. If you forget it, email us and we will enable a reset.

We may suspend an account that is being used to store something other than business receipts, or in a way that threatens the service for other people.

What you upload

Upload receipts and documents that are yours to keep. Files are limited to 8 MB each, as photos or PDFs.

Availability

We will do our best to keep the tool running and your files intact, but it is provided as it is, without a guarantee of availability. If we ever need to shut it down, we will email you first so you can take your files.
